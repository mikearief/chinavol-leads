Us Session Recap

SPY closed at 746.74, QQQ at 740.62 — elevated but volatile. The June 17 FOMC held rates at 3.50-3.75% unanimously, with Warsh dropping forward guidance entirely and launching five task forces to rethink the Fed's framework. The dot plot tells the story: 9 of 18 members want rate hikes by year-end, median 2026 PCE inflation raised sharply to 3.6% from 2.7% in March. The Iran-driven oil shock is the new variable — energy supply disruptions feeding through to CPI. VIX at 16.78 says markets are pricing a soft landing, but the 10Y yield at 4.45% and the hawkish tilt suggest bond traders are nervous. Warsh's "we will deliver price stability" is the most hawkish Fed rhetoric in years — markets haven't fully priced what a rate hike cycle would mean.

China Watch

FXI at 33.30, KWEB at 25.24 — both range-bound. The real action is structural: China's $1.2T trade surplus is redirecting from the US (exports down 37% YoY Jan-Apr) to Europe (exports up 16.4% Jan-May), triggering "China Shock 2.0" panic at the G7 in Évian. France's Macron says Chinese exports are "literally killing" European industry. The US "Board of Trade" mechanism with China is agreed in principle but cuts haven't taken effect — July 24 is the deadline for the Section 301 overcapacity investigation spanning 16 economies. On the ground: BBVA forecasts 4.5% GDP, PBoC on hold with no RRR or rate cuts expected, USD/CNY broke below 6.80 on yuan appreciation. PPI turned positive for the first time in 41 months (+2.8% YoY April) — but it's imported oil inflation, not demand recovery. Shenzhen Component surged 7.13%, ChiNext +11.02% last week — tech/AI rotation alive. Summer Davos in Tianjin June 23-26 is the near-term catalyst watch.

Risk Flag

Iran/Hormuz escalation is the tail risk nobody is hedging — Iran's oil production collapsed 28% since February per OPEC data, UAE has left OPEC, and the US-Iran peace talks just suspended. Oil at 77.52 could spike to $100+ on any Strait closure, which would force Warsh to hike and crush equity multiples.

Trade Signal

Long KWEB over FXI. Chinese internet names benefit from PBoC easing optionality (10-20bps asymmetric cut possible in H2), yuan appreciation reducing capital outflow risk, and AI supply chain earnings momentum — while FXI's financial-heavy composition gets squeezed by bank NIMs at historical lows (1.40%). KWEB at 25.24 offers better risk/reward than the heavily financial large-cap ETF. Avoid commodities here — oil at 77.52 is a geopolitical trade, not a fundamental one, and OPEC+ quota hikes are flooding supply even as Iran drops out.
