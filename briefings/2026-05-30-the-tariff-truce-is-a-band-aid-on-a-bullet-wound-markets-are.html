The tariff truce is a Band-Aid on a bullet wound — markets are pricing détente but structural de-coupling is accelerating.

2026-05-30 · Pre-Market Alert
SPY QQQ FXI KWEB

Us Session Recap

SPY closed at 756.48, QQQ at 738.31 — both grinding higher on the extended 90-day truce and post-summit "stabilization" narrative. But the VIX at 15.32 isn't complacent; it's elevated for a market at all-time highs. The 10Y at 4.45% is the real story — the Fed held at 3.75% with inflation stuck at 3.8%, meaning real rates are still negative. New Fed Chair Warsh inherits sticky inflation, and the April FOMC minutes showed the modal path is two 25bp cuts over 12 months — but war-related inflation risks keep pushing the timeline.

China Watch

The yuan hit a 3-year high in mid-May on the trade truce and $17B in deal announcements. FXI at 35.05 and KWEB at 26.73 have rallied hard on the "stabilization" narrative. But traders are missing the critical point: China's export machine just absorbed ~$50B in additional tariff damage before the truce. Those lost orders don't come back. Yuan strength is also eating into export margins — Beijing faces a policy trilemma: defend the yuan, support exports, or stimulate domestic demand. They cannot do all three.

Risk Flag

The oil market is pricing $87 WTI with an embedded Iran war premium of $15-20/bbl — when that premium evaporates (ceasefire or status quo freeze), crude could dump 20% and take EM currencies, including the yuan, with it.

Trade Signal

Short KWEB, long FXI. KWEB's tech-heavy composition is exposed to the semiconductor decoupling nobody is hedging, while FXI's state-owned heavyweights get direct PBOC support. Yuan strength favors A-shares over ADRs. If the macro trade sours, state-owned dividends cushion FXI; KWEB takes it on the chin.

今日要点: The truce is priced; the decoupling isn't. Position for the divergence nobody is talking about.
