China's economy is bifurcating in real time — the AI export boom masks the first domestic retail contraction in three years, while the US-Iran peace deal crashes oil and resets the macro playbook for H2.

2026-06-21 · Pre-Market Alert
SPY QQQ FXI KWEB

Us Session Recap

SPY closed at $746.74, QQQ at $740.62 — both hovering near all-time highs as the VIX sits at 16.78, complacent for a market with this much cross-current. The June 17 FOMC was the real event: Warsh held rates at 3.50–3.75%, dropped forward guidance entirely, and 9 of 18 members now pencil in a hike by year-end. PCE inflation forecast was jacked from 2.7% to 3.6%, with core at 3.3%. The Fed admits energy supply shocks from the Iran war are the driver, but Warsh's "inflation is a choice" line tells you where they lean. The 10-year yield at 4.45% is not pricing cuts anytime soon.

China Watch

The divergence is stark and widening. Exports surged 14.7% YoY in Q1, with May semiconductor exports up 110% and auto exports +75% — the AI and clean-energy supercycle is real. Goldman raised their CSI 300 target to 5,500 and sees A-share EPS growth at 20%. But on the other side: May retail sales contracted -0.6% — the first monthly decline in over three years. The trade-in subsidy program is exhausted. Gasoline costs are up 20%+ from the oil shock, squeezing household budgets. The tariff truce with the US is holding at ~30% effective rates, with China buying 200 Boeing jets and $17B/yr in agricultural goods to extend the détente past November. The yuan is strong at 6.9, appreciating 1.2% YTD. FXI at $33.30, KWEB at $25.24. Trivium China's call is right: one economy, two completely different trajectories.

Risk Flag

The AI capex cycle is concentrated in a handful of hyperscalers — if it plateaus (as every tech cycle eventually does), China loses its primary demand engine with no domestic consumption buffer to fall back on. Retail contraction + 5.4% unemployment + fragile consumer confidence = no safety net.

Trade Signal

Long KWEB / short China consumer. Buy KWEB on dips below $24.50. The export/tech narrative is backed by real order books and policy tailwinds (DeepSeek raising $7.4B, AI infrastructure buildout). The domestic consumption weakness is structural and will take years to fix — don't fight the bifurcation. Meanwhile, oil at $76.54 (WTI) is down 8% this week on the US-Iran peace framework and Strait of Hormuz reopening, but gold at $4,172 says inflation fears aren't priced out yet. Brent at $80.57 — expect whipsaw as 500+ stranded tankers clear and Kuwait/Iraq ramp back 2M+ bpd.

今日要点: Bet on the divergence — long China exporters and AI plays, avoid domestic consumer exposure. The bifurcation is the trade of H2.
