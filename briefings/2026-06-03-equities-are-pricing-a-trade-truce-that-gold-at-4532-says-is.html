China Watch

But Xi's retaliatory statecraft was a master class: a $1T+ trade surplus, rare earth leverage used with surgical precision, and two auto-industry shutdowns that proved dependency on Chinese inputs is the real vulnerability. FXI at 36.53 and KWEB at 28.49 look cheap, but "managed decoupling" means the rerating premium China stocks once held is gone permanently. The internal picture is worse — retail sales slowing, property still broken, credit stress at local governments.
