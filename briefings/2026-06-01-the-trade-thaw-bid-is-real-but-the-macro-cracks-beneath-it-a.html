Us Session Recap

SPY closed at $756.48 with VIX at a complacent 15.32 — the buy-everything trade is running on autopilot. QQQ at $738.31, 10Y yield at 4.45%. The X discourse is split: half the timeline is celebrating the Trump-Xi trade truce, the other half is flagging the Q2 inflation print at 6% and the Fed pushed to the late 2026 corner. The bond market isn't buying the soft landing — yields sitting stubbornly above 4.4% tell you the sticky inflation story isn't going away.

China Watch

FXI at $35.05, KWEB at $26.73 — China equities are pricing the tariff cuts but not the domestic rot. New yuan loans shrank by ¥10B in April — a contraction. That's a credit impulse turning negative at a time when the PBOC should be easing. The yuan hit a 3-year high against the dollar, but that's a function of a weak USD narrative, not Chinese demand strength. A-share market cap above ¥110T looks stretched against contracting credit. The tariff détente is the sugar high — the real catalyst for Chinese equities is whether Beijing can reflate domestic demand without the export crutch.

Risk Flag

The biggest blind spot is oil — CL=F at $89.47 after crashing 17% in May. If this is a demand-driven collapse (and the EIA still forecasting $106 Brent looks increasingly delusional), it's a deflationary signal that blows up the "immaculate disinflation" thesis and pressures EM credit, including China's property sector.

Trade Signal

Long KWEB below $27 with a stop at $24.50. The tariff détente is a genuine catalyst, but the thesis fails if China credit doesn't stabilize. If FXI breaks $36 on volume, add size. Short crude on any bounce above $95 — the EIA is behind the curve and OPEC's discipline is cracking.
