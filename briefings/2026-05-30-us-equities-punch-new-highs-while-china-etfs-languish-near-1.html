US equities punch new highs while China ETFs languish near 12-month lows — the trade détente narrative is priced into SPY, priced out of FXI.

2026-05-30 · Pre-Market Alert
SPY FXI QQQ KWEB

Us Session Recap

SPY closed at $756.48 on May 29, up 28% from a year ago and just off all-time highs. The VIX slumped to 15.33 — that's complacent territory for a market facing 3.8% core inflation, a Fed stuck at 3.75% with zero cuts priced in for 2026, and Brent oil still near $97. The 10-year yield at 4.45% is a constant reminder that the "no landing" scenario is alive and well. Tech led again — QQQ at $741, up 2.3% in the last week — but breadth is narrowing. This has the fingerprints of a momentum-driven melt-up, not a broad-based recovery.

China Watch

FXI ($35.05) and KWEB (~$26.60) are trading as if trade talks never happened. That's the opportunity. The US is seeking public comment on tariff cuts for Chinese goods, China has signaled willingness to accept some tariffs within the Malaysia framework, and agricultural tariff cuts are already on the table. None of this goodwill is reflected in China ETF pricing. The Shanghai Composite at 4,099 is the outlier — domestic A-shares are holding up much better than the US-listed China names. JPMorgan just upgraded China equities on 13% earnings growth expectations. The dollar-yuan dynamic is the wildcard: if Beijing lets the yuan slide to offset tariff pain, the FXI/KWEB thesis gets complicated. But if the yuan stabilizes on a deal, the compression trade fires hard.

Risk Flag

The biggest risk is that the tariff détente is a mirage — the US and China fundamentally disagree on industrial policy, and any "deal" that leaves semiconductor/SW barriers in place will disappoint markets hoping for a full reset.

Trade Signal

Buy KWEB puts or short KWEB at $27.50+ for a tactical hedge — but the real asymmetric bet is long FXI calls for August expiry. The risk/reward on China reopening upside from $35 is the best in developed markets. If trade talks progress, FXI goes to $40+ in weeks. If they collapse, it tests $34. You lose a dollar. You make five. Take that bet.

今日要点

The market is pricing a trade deal in SPY and a trade war in FXI. One of them is wrong. Bet against the crowd.
