## 🔴 THE ALERT (full text)

Us Session Recap

SPY closed at 757.09, QQQ at 740.61 — both at/near record levels — with VIX pinned at 15.40 despite the US-Iran conflict and a 145% tariff wall on Chinese goods. The 10-year yield at 4.48% tells the real story: the bond market is not buying the dovish pivot. JPMorgan's latest call says the Fed's next move is a hike, not a cut. Polymarket gives 61% odds of no change at the June FOMC. The equity bid is pure tariff-peace euphoria — there's no fundamental cover for VIX at 15 with this macro backdrop.

China Watch

US-China trade talks are active — the Busan truce framework has tariffs down from the 145%/125% apex but the structural issues (supply chains, rare earths, tech decoupling) remain untouched. Trump's Beijing meeting with Xi was the headline event in May; the yuan hit a 3-year high on the optimism. But the Shanghai Composite is at 4058, down from its recent peak — domestic investors are selling the summit news. KWEB at 27.13 and FXI at 35.47 have priced in a partial deal but not a full reset. The wildcard: China's Anti-Sanctions Law, invoked in May to fight US blacklisting of refiners buying Iranian oil. Beijing is building legal weapons, not de-escalating. Record A-share volume (2.8 trillion yuan in a single day) tells you retail is piling in — but that's usually a top signal in China.

Risk Flag

Gold at $4,480 and rising while VIX sits at 15 is the widest risk-bid/equity-complacency gap in a decade — if the trade talks collapse or the Iran situation escalates, vol explodes and gold smashes through $5,000.

Trade Signal

Buy GC=F (gold), short SPY. The gold/SPY ratio is screaming regime change. SPY at 757 assumes tariff cuts and a Fed cut. Gold at $4,480 assumes neither. One of these is wrong, and gold has been right about every macro dislocation since 2022. Short SPY as a tail hedge, stay long gold as the core view. If the deal happens, gold dips but the hedge was justified; if talks collapse, gold rallies hard and SPY bleeds.
