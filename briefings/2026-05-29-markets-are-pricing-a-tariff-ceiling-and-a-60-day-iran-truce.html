Markets are pricing a "tariff ceiling" and a 60-day Iran truce, but the gap between equity complacency and the bond market's inflation reality is the widest it's been all year.

2026-05-29 · Pre-Market Alert
SPY QQQ KWEB FXI

Us Session Recap

SPY closed at 754.60, QQQ at 735.60 — grinding higher on the Iran ceasefire extension narrative. The White House confirmed a 60-day MOU to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, but Trump has yet to formally sign off. The real story is in rates: 10Y at 4.45% and a Reuters poll showing 85% of economists see the Fed on hold through Q3 — with futures now pricing in a 25bp HIKE by January 2027. VIX at 15.74 is pricing zero tail risk. That's the trade setup right there: equities are complacent, bonds are screaming.

China Watch

The Shanghai Composite (+22% YoY) and CSI 300 (+28% YoY) are on a tear — industrial profits up 18.2% Jan-Apr, and the LPR stuck at a record-low 3.00% for 12 straight months tells you Beijing is all-in on liquidity support. The big catalyst? BYD unveiled the Xuanji A3, China's first automotive-grade 4nm autonomous driving chip. This is a domestic semiconductor validation event — BYD now owns the full stack from chip to vehicle. On trade, Bessent said "things are stable" and the US is in no rush to extend the one-year truce. The effective tariff rate on China settled at ~29.7% (down from the 36.8% peak), and the Section 122 global tariff (10%) expires July 24. Any failure to renew is a positive tailwind for Chinese exports. The yuan is setting up for a second monthly gain — capital is rotating back in.

Risk Flag

The Iran ceasefire is a 60-day MOU that Trump hasn't signed — if it collapses, oil ($87.74 WTI) rips toward $106+ (EIA's Brent forecast), and the VIX spike would shred this rally. That's the asymmetric tail.

Trade Signal

Long KWEB / FXI on dips. The tariff ceiling is in, domestic tech is accelerating (BYD chip, 18% profit growth), and Chinese equities are still trading at a 40% P/E discount to the S&P. The July 24 global tariff expiration is a call option on further de-escalation. Pair with buying VIX calls at 17 strike — the complacency trade is the crowded one.
