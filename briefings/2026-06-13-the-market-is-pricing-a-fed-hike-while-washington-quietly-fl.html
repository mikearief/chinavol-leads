Us Session Recap

Bond traders are now pricing a rate HIKE before year-end after the latest CPI print — Goldman Sachs, Forbes, and a Reuters economist poll all say no cuts in 2026. CNBC reports Fed officials are openly discussing rate hikes, while WaPo flags the new Fed chief may be forced to defy Trump and raise. The market is not pricing this in fully yet — that's the gap.

China Watch

The biggest story is the US seeking public comment on Chinese goods eligible for tariff REDUCTIONS — a genuine de-escalation signal from the Commerce Department post-Trump-Xi summit. KWEB at 26.49 and FXI at 35.29 are pricing some of this, but not all. Meanwhile, CNBC reports China's stock market is "overheating" with record turnover, and regulators are getting worried. Global investors are rotating into A-shares and the yuan (Bloomberg calls it the "big bet for 2026"). Yuan rally is fueling the inflow — overseas investors now hold over 4 trillion yuan in A-share free float. The tension: tariff relief bullish, but Beijing may step in to cool the rally.

Risk Flag

Fed hike risk is the single biggest unhedged tail risk — if a hike comes, every risk asset from KWEB to BTC (63,481) to EM equities gets hammered, and the tariff relief narrative gets drowned out by dollar strength.
