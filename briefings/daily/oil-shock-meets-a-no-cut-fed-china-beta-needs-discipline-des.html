Gold remains the macro hedge to watch alongside the higher VIX, while copper is the cleaner China-demand barometer; today’s stronger oil move is geopolitical and supply-led rather than a synchronized industrial-demand confirmation. If crude strength persists while A-shares open flat, airlines, chemicals, and consumer cyclicals face margin pressure, while upstream energy and commodity-linked SOEs keep relative support.

Taiwan/China Polymarket risk did not register as an elevated top-200 sentinel in today’s run, but X discourse around gray-zone maritime pressure and energy-route vulnerability keeps Taiwan risk relevant for semis, shipping, insurance, and oil-sensitive China sectors.
