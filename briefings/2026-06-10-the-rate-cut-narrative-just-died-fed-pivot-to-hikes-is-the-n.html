China Watch

The PBOC has room to ease while the Fed tightens — that policy divergence is a tailwind for Chinese equities versus EM peers. X traders flagging that KWEB's valuation (16x forward earnings) prices in a recession that isn't coming.

Risk Flag

The biggest risk is a July Fed hike — markets have zero rate hikes priced but BNP and Yardeni say the bond vigilantes will force the Fed's hand if core inflation stays above 3%.

Trade Signal

Long FXI July $35 calls. The consensus is too bearish on China. US rate hikes hurt EM broadly, but China's domestic stimulus cycle is independent. FXI at $34.59 with the PBOC easing is a better risk/reward than fighting the Fed in US equities. Entry at $34.50, stop at $33, target $37.50.
