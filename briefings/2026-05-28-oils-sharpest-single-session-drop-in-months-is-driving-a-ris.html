Us Session Recap

X traders are buzzing about the sudden oil collapse — WTI dropped ~5% to ~$89 on reports of a potential US-Iran framework deal unlocking Strait of Hormuz shipping. Energy stocks (XLE, OIH) got hammered, but equities caught a bid on the inflation relief thesis — lower energy = lower CPI = Fed pivot hope rekindling. SPY held firm near 749, QQQ flat at 727. VIX compressed to 17. This feels like a relief rally masking internals that aren't working. Breadth under the surface is ugly.

China Watch

The $200 billion A-share wipeout isn't a capitulation — it's a rotation. X analysts are flagging that the yuan just hit its strongest level versus the USD in over a year while 4,000 stocks bled red. That disconnect is the tell: capital is exiting equities but staying inside China's financial system. Goldman Sachs data going viral on X shows yuan usage in China-linked trade surged from 13% to 30% since 2019 — de-dollarization is accelerating whether Western observers are watching or not.

Industrial profits surged +24.7% YoY in April — the fastest pace in two years — driven by export manufacturing and PPI recovery. The Fed's "higher for longer" backdrop keeps yuan strength sustainable as PBOC has room to hold rates elevated without crushing growth. Tariff reduction talks on ~$30B of non-strategic goods are noise; EVs, chips, and rare earths face near-total effective blockage. Xi told Trump directly: Taiwan is a red line.

Watch: FXI (35.35) testing support. KWEB (27.10) following tech rotation. If yuan holds strength with equities weak, PBOC has room for targeted A-share stimulus without igniting inflation.

Risk Flag

US-Iran deal execution risk is high — Hormuz reopening in ~30 days would crater energy further and reshuffle global shipping/inflation dynamics overnight.

Trade Signal

Short oil (CL) into any Iran-deal confirmation. Long China semis/tech on A-share weakness — the sector that showed gains in today's bloodbath is where Beijing's strategic capital is hiding. Avoid KWEB momentum until FXI stabilizes — passive flows follow, they don't lead.

China's stock-currency divergence is a feature, not a bug — strong yuan gives PBOC the room to selectively prop tech while the rest of A-shares resets.
