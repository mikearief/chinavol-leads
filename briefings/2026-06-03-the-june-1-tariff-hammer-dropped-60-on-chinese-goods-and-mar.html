Us Session Recap

SPY closed at 759.57, grinding higher despite the June 1 tariff escalation going live. China Watch

The 60% tariff is real, but the market is reading the nuance: chip tariffs were pushed to mid-2027, and Hassett's $2K rebate talk signals the White House knows the consumer pain is coming. Yuan at 6.76 is a stealth bullish signal — China is letting the currency absorb some of the tariff shock rather than devaluing, a confidence move that Goldman and Bernstein are buying. FXI at 36.36 and KWEB at 28.33 haven't priced the global fund rotation yet. The Shanghai Composite at 4075 is up 0.43% — China equities are holding up better than they should if the tariff was truly devastating.

Risk Flag

The VIX at 15.77 vs gold at $4,519 is the widest risk-asset divergence of 2026 — one of them is lying, and it's not gold.

Trade Signal

Long KWEB, short SPY. The risk/reward asymmetry is stark: KWEB at 28.33 is pricing total decoupling, but the yuan strength and Goldman inflows say otherwise. If the FOMC holds steady or sounds dovish, EM growth stories re-rate. If tariffs escalate further, SPY takes the hit harder than China tech — the supply chains have already rerouted.
