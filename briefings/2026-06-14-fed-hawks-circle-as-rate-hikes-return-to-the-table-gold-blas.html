Fed hawks circle as rate hikes return to the table — gold blasts through $4,200 while trade war widens to 60 nations.

2026-06-14 · Pre-Market Alert
SPY QQQ FXI KWEB

Us Session Recap

US equities eked out gains Friday (SPY +0.54% to $741.75, QQQ +0.59% to $721.34) but the real action was in rates and commodities. The 10Y yield sits at 4.49% with VIX at 17.68 — elevated but not broken. Gold surged 3.63% to $4,238.80. The catalyst: Kevin Warsh's first FOMC meeting starts this week. Washington Post reports the new Fed chief "may soon be forced to defy Trump and raise rates." Nomura has already abandoned its rate-cut call. Markets that spent all 2025 pricing cuts are waking up to a hike cycle nobody wants to talk about.

China Watch

FXI rallied 1.09% to $35.29 — yuan strength is the silent driver. The yuan has been on a tear, pushing Shanghai comp to decade highs. But KWEB slipped 0.30% to $26.49, and that divergence tells everything. The forced-labor tariff front the US just opened against 60 economies + the EU is broader than the 2018 trade war — it's a multilateral play. Beijing will retaliate, and consumer tech takes the first hit. Yuan strength is double-edged: great for inflows, painful for export-heavy A-shares.

Risk Flag

Fed hiking into a trade-war slowdown is the stagflation playbook. The Iran war just crossed day 100 — OPEC+ has hiked output 4x since Hormuz closure, but oil infrastructure risk is not priced out. If oil reverses, the Gold-Treasury-Equity triangle snaps.

Trade Signal

Buy gold, short China tech. Gold at $4,238 is breaking out on real-rate repricing + geopolitical bid. KWEB has no business rallying into a 60-country tariff escalation. Long FXI for yuan/value exposure, hedge with KWEB puts. Crude at $84.88 after a 3.23% dump is a watch — OPEC+ demand cuts are contrarian buys.

今日要点: The market is pricing rate hikes it doesn't believe yet. That's the opportunity — buy the hedge (gold) before reality sets in.
