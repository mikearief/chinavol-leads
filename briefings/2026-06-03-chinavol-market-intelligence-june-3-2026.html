Headline: The June 1 tariff hammer dropped — 60% on Chinese goods — and markets yawned. That calm before the FOMC storm is the real signal.

US Session Recap: SPY closed at 759.57, grinding higher despite the June 1 tariff escalation going live. QQQ at 746.16 is holding tech together, but the internals are brittle — VIX at 15.77 is too low for a market that just absorbed the broadest China tariff since Smoot-Hawley. The 10Y at 4.45% confirms the bond market isn't buying the "tariff peak" narrative. FOMC June 16-17 is live — no cut expected, but the dot plot will reveal whether the tariff-induced inflation scare is forcing another hawkish pivot.

China Watch: The 60% tariff is real, but the market is reading the nuance: chip tariffs were pushed to mid-2027, and Hassett's $2K rebate talk signals the White House knows the consumer pain is coming. Yuan at 6.76 is a stealth bullish signal — China is letting the currency absorb some of the tariff shock rather than devaluing, a confidence move that Goldman and Bernstein are buying. FXI at 36.36 and KWEB at 28.33 haven't priced the global fund rotation yet. The Shanghai Composite at 4075 is up 0.43% — China equities are holding up better than they should if the tariff was truly devastating.

Risk Flag: The VIX at 15.77 vs gold at $4,519 is the widest risk-asset divergence of 2026 — one of them is lying, and it's not gold.

Trade Signal: Long KWEB, short SPY. The risk/reward asymmetry is stark: KWEB at 28.33 is pricing total decoupling, but the yuan strength and Goldman inflows say otherwise. If the FOMC holds steady or sounds dovish, EM growth stories re-rate. If tariffs escalate further, SPY takes the hit harder than China tech — the supply chains have already rerouted.

今日要点: The tariff escalation everyone feared just landed — and the market shrugged. That tells you the real risk is the Fed, not Beijing.
