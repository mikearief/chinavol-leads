Oil peace plunge meets PCE-day anxiety — the tariff détente is real but markets are looking at the wrong risk.

2026-05-28 · Pre-Market Alert
FXI KWEB CL=F

Us Session Recap

US equities rallied Tuesday (S&P +0.6%, Nasdaq +1.2%) on tech strength — Micron surging 19% past $1T market cap after Barclays tripled its target, and NVDA pledging $150B to Taiwan chip capacity. WTI collapsed 5.7% to $88.50 on Iranian signals the Strait of Hormuz could reopen within a month. But the macro picture is deteriorating beneath the rally: the 10Y at 4.48%, the Conference Board confirmed two-thirds of consumers are cutting spending on fuel costs, and the market is now pricing a Fed HIKE by January 2027. Pre-market is soft — ZS cratering 25% on weak guidance, dragging cybersecurity names.

China Watch

This is the real story. USTR Greer announced yesterday (May 27) the US will seek public comment on which Chinese goods qualify for tariff cuts — the first concrete administrative step toward de-escalation since the Xi-Trump summit's handshake agreements. Reports peg the scope at ~$30B in potential reductions. FXI ($35.32) and KWEB ($27.04) are barely pricing this in. Meanwhile, China's new critical minerals law fully nationalizing supply chain control from mine to market proves Beijing is building parallel infrastructure regardless of trade détente. Yuan at 3-year highs — the PBJ is comfortable letting it appreciate to offset import inflation from oil.

Risk Flag

The April PCE print drops today — consensus expects +0.3% core m/m, but upstream producer prices are surging from the Iran oil spike. A hot core print (0.4%+) immediately repaves the path toward a Fed hike, crushing the rate-sensitive tech rally and sending the 10Y toward 4.7%. VIX at 16.3 says nobody is hedging.

Trade Signal

Long FXI, short CL=F. The tariff-rollback public comment process is a genuine catalyst for Chinese equities that the market is flat-out ignoring — FXI at $35 prices zero de-escalation premium despite the most concrete US administrative move in months. On the flip side, WTI at $90 prices maximum Hormuz disruption risk that is rapidly unwinding on peace talks. If Iran delivers on the Strait reopening within a month, the oil risk premium collapses $10-15. Pair trade: buy China reopening / short crude.
