Headline: The US just proposed tariffs on 60 economies including allies — this is the trade war going global, and markets have no idea what's coming.

US Session Recap: SPY sits at 755.15 after nine straight green days — a streak built on AI euphoria and benign macro data (ISM Manufacturing hit 54, beating 53 consensus). But the USTR dropped a Section 301 bomb June 3: 10–12.5% tariffs on 60 economies including the UK, EU, Canada, India, Japan, and China for "failure to enforce forced labour import prohibitions." Public comments due July 6, tariffs could lock in by late July. VIX at 16.29 is still pricing this as noise. That is a mistake.

China Watch: Beijing already faces an average ~50% tariff rate, so another 12.5% is incremental — but the narrative is devastating. The US is no longer fighting a bilateral trade war with China; it's building a global tariff architecture that isolates China entirely. The yuan has been strengthening past 7/USD on capital inflows and Goldman/Bernstein upgrades, but this weaponization of Section 301 against the entire G20 means yuan appreciation just became a headwind for Chinese exports at exactly the wrong moment. FXI at 35.62 and KWEB at 27.33 are not pricing any of this.

Risk Flag: The VIX at 16.29 is pricing a benign world while the US simultaneously prosecutes a hot war with Iran (WTI $95.95, Strait of Hormuz contested) and expands tariff hostilities to every major trading partner — when this volatility suppression breaks, the re-pricing will cascade across FX, rates, and equities.

Trade Signal: SHORT SPY, LONG KWEB. The S&P 500's nine-day win streak is built on a policy calm that just shattered. KWEB at 27.33 has been so relentlessly sold that another 12.5% tariff is already baked in — the actual trade war expansion hits US multinationals harder than Chinese ADRs that already trade at a 40% discount to global peers. Target SPY 730, KWEB 32. Stop on SPY at 765.
