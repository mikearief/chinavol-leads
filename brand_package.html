Usage Guidelines

  • Icon: Use at 32×32 (favicon), 96×96 (social), 256×256 (profile) and up. Crop to circle or rounded square for avatars.
  • Wordmark: Inter 900. All body/text on the site uses Inter 400–700. No other font for brand lockup.
  • Tagline: "Volatility Intelligence" — uppercase, letter-spacing 0.3em, 13–14px, secondary text color.
  • Background: Use darkest navy (#0a1628) behind the icon. For light backgrounds, invert the icon to a white or gold outline.
  • Clearspace: Keep at least the height of the "C" in ChinaVol as empty space around the logo on all sides.
  • Social:>/strong> For 𝕏/Discord avatars, scale icon to 400×400 with 4px rounded corners, fill background #0a1628.
